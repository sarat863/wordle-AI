# 🎮 Wordle AI User Guide

Welcome to **Wordle AI**! This guide covers everything you need to know about playing, tracking your stats, using AI hints, and competing with friends.

---

## 🕹️ Game Modes

1. **♾️ Practice Mode (5 Letters)**: Unlimited puzzles to sharpen your vocabulary.
2. **📅 Daily Challenge**: One universal word per calendar day. Everyone gets the same word! Build and maintain your daily streak.
3. **⚡ Speed Run (3 Minutes)**: Race against the countdown timer to test your reflexes and speed.
4. **4️⃣ 4-Letter Mini Mode**: Fast-paced 4-letter word puzzles.
5. **6️⃣ 6-Letter Master Mode**: Challenging 6-letter words for expert players.
6. **🔗 Friend Challenge**: Create a custom secret word, generate an encrypted challenge link, and send it to your friends.

---

## 🎨 Color Coding Rules

- 🟩 **Green (Correct)**: The letter is in the correct position.
- 🟨 **Yellow (Present)**: The letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
- ⬛ **Gray (Absent)**: The letter is not in the word at all.

---

## 🧠 Using the AI Solver Drawer

Click the **AI Solver** button on the top navigation bar to open the AI assistant:
- **Optimal Moves**: Shows top recommended words ranked by Shannon Entropy (bits of information).
- **Smart Clues**: Get progressive hints on vowel counts and unrevealed letters without spoiling the game.
- **Word Analyzer**: Test any word to calculate its mathematical information value.

---

## ♿ Accessibility & Customization

Open **Settings** (⚙️) to:
- Turn on **High Contrast Mode** (Orange & Sky Blue tiles for color vision accessibility).
- Toggle between **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**.
- Enable or disable **Synthesized Audio Effects**.
- Activate **Hard Mode** for extra challenge and bonus points.
